Chapter 01 — Sense CHM-01

Expanded Rectangular Chamber & Sensing

Wide rectangular food-grade brushed metallic liner with a dual-zone cold-chain layout stocked across both the left wire basket and right clinical specimen bay.

Spanning two-thirds of the rectangular enclosure width, the deep stainless-steel and anodized aluminium chamber is surrounded by multi-layer thermal insulation. The left half houses a removable wire basket stocked with vaccine cryo-vials, amber serum bottles, and biologics kits, while the right half accommodates a clinical organizer tray with borosilicate plasma carboys, an mRNA cryo-carrier, a 15-tube vacutainer rack, and phase-change cooling bricks—all actively cooled by circulating blue cold air and monitored by the wall-mounted temperature/humidity probe.

Chapter 03 — Power VRFB-03

Vanadium Redox Flow Battery (VRFB)

Paired positive and negative aqueous electrolyte reservoirs with circulation pumps and a central electrochemical cell stack.

Two dedicated tanks store positive (red circuit) and negative (blue circuit) liquid electrolytes. Twin micro-pumps drive electrolyte through continuous curved manifolds into the central flow-cell stack and back via return lines. Because energy is stored in aqueous vanadium electrolytes, VRFB architecture offers long cycle life, non-flammable aqueous chemistry, and strong potential for electrolyte reuse—while recognizing that overall environmental impact depends on material sourcing, manufacturing, operation, and end-of-life management.

Positive LoopRed-Indexed Reservoir + Pump + Stack Port
Negative LoopBlue-Indexed Reservoir + Pump + Stack Port
Sustainability NoteReusable Aqueous Electrolyte Chemistry
Chapter 04 — Cool TEC-04

Peltier Thermoelectric Cooling Stack

Strict top-to-bottom solid-state heat pumping: cold absorption above, thermoelectric wafer in the middle, heat rejection below.

When DC power is applied to the central Peltier thermoelectric module, heat is absorbed at the upper blue cold-side finned heat exchanger and pumped downward into the hot-side copper spreader and lower hot-side finned heat sink. Dual rotary exhaust fans draw warm waste air strictly from the lower hot-side sink and expel it through the enclosure vents.

Chapter 05 — Sterilise UVC-05

Global 369 UV-C Sterilisation Module

Wide under-lid germicidal UV-C emitter with protective cage, umbilical hinge harness, and continuous open/closed illumination mode.

Mounted beneath the inner liner of the insulated lid and spanning the rectangular storage chamber, the Global 369 UV-C emitter projects ultraviolet light from its luminous violet core, outer halo tube, and volumetric irradiation cone. For full visual inspection, the UV-C module remains actively illuminated when toggled ON even while the lid is open at any angle (0° to 68°). UV-C light damages microbial DNA/RNA to inactivate susceptible surface pathogens depending on dose, exposure time, distance, and line-of-sight.
